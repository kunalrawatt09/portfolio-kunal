# Handcrafted Portfolio Redesign

## Direction
Rebuild the existing portfolio as a Swiss editorial personal site: warm paper, black ink, one red accent, strict grid, strong typography, and small human details inspired by a printed résumé and working notebook.

## What will change
- Replace the dramatic glowing hero with a clean editorial introduction that immediately presents Kunal, his role, availability, and portrait.
- Recompose projects as numbered case studies with asymmetric image/text layouts, visible project facts, and clear external links.
- Redesign About, Skills, and Certifications as spacious, structured sections rather than repeated cards.
- Simplify navigation, contact, motion, borders, and responsive behavior so the site feels authored rather than generated.
- Preserve all current project, skill, certificate, contact, and verification content.

## Technical details
- Update the single portfolio route and shared design tokens/font loading only.
- Keep imagery local and preserve accessibility, SEO metadata, mobile layouts, and live India time.
- Validate the final page at desktop and mobile widths and fix any visual or build errors.
